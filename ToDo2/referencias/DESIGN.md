## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, serene, and disciplined environment for daily life architecture and personal mastery. Grounded in Nordic minimalism, Swiss typographic restraint, and contemporary editorial publication sensibilities, the interface treats personal productivity not as an overwhelming mechanical conveyor belt, but as a deliberate and mindful daily ritual.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** High-performing professionals, knowledge workers, creators, and design-conscious individuals who value clarity, focus, and deliberate pacing over aggressive gamification.
- **Emotional Response:** Quiet competence, mental clarity, tactile satisfaction, and unhurried composure. The UI retreats into the background to elevate the user's thoughts and routines.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Papercraft meets Digital Precision:** Pure white surfaces resting over an off-white atmospheric canvas, grounded by crisp hairline framing, expansive whitespace, and nuanced typography.
- **Restraint Over Decoration:** Vibrancy is deployed sparingly for meaningful state changes, task completion, and vital tags, ensuring that high-contrast charcoal text commands primary focus.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture balances an airy neutral canvas with deeply authoritative charcoal tones and selective botanical and earthen accents. Every color serves functional legibility and emotional equilibrium.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas:** `#F9F9FB` — A warm, luminous off-white creating a soft optical cushion that eliminates eye fatigue.
- **Elevated Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` — Pure white for structural cards, sheets, and modular panels to create organic layering without stark tonal breaks.
- **Hairline Dividers & Outlines:** `#E8E8ED` or `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.06)` — Crisp, nearly imperceptible borders that provide physical definition to elements without cluttering the screen.

### Text & Typographic Hierarchy
- **Text Strong / Primary:** `#121316` — Deep, near-black charcoal providing pristine contrast for editorial headings, task titles, and dominant numbers.
- **Text Muted / Body:** `#5E636E` — Balanced mid-tone slate for supporting descriptions, subheaders, and notes.
- **Text Subtle / Metadata:** `#9499A5` — Light neutral for timestamps, auxiliary counters, empty-state placeholders, and inactive icons.

### Accent & Semantic Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#1E293B`):** Deep editorial slate used for key actions, solid primary buttons, active pill backgrounds, and anchoring highlights.
- **Botanical Sage (`#2A7E5D`):** Organic sage green for completed states, streak progress, milestone achievements, and positive balance.
- **Warm Terracotta (`#C97A3E`):** Earthen amber for priority flags, urgent calendar commitments, and critical alerts without the harshness of raw traffic reds.
- **Soft Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Subdued indigo reserved exclusively for contextual list grouping, project categories, and personal tags.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence reminiscent of high-end architectural periodicals. Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean geometric hulls with humanized, friendly counters, avoiding the sterile clinical feeling of standard enterprise typefaces.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tighter Tracking on Displays:** Large headings use negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to create a cohesive, authoritative editorial punch.
- **Airy Microcopy:** Small labels and metadata leverage expanded letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.08em`) to ensure instant glanceability at reduced sizes.
- **Hierarchy through Weight & Color:** Emphasize critical tasks and daily agendas by shifting from `Text Strong` to `Text Muted` rather than escalating font size unnecessarily.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a dynamic fluid column system bounded by safe zones and generous breathing room. The visual weight relies on structured vertical rhythm rather than cluttered gridlines.

### Mobile Grid & Architecture
- **Primary Grid:** 4-column layout on mobile devices (`<640px`) transitioning to 8 columns on tablet frames (`640px - 1024px`).
- **Canvas Margins:** 16px (`1rem`) on compact displays expanding to 20px (`1.25rem`) on modern standard devices, ensuring content rests comfortably away from screen bezels.
- **Vertical Flow:** Spacing follows a modular 4px base unit. Sections, groupings, and list blocks utilize larger separators (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) to evoke spacious magazine layouts.
- **Touch Targets:** Every interactive item maintains a minimum footprint of 44×44px, irrespective of its visual perimeter.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft ambient diffusion and translucent material physics, avoiding muddy, high-contrast drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Structure
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Sitting flush on `#F9F9FB`. Used for inline text fields, passive list rows, and structural separators.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a delicate hairline border (`1px solid rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.05)`) and an ultra-diffused ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.02), 0 8px 24px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.04);`
- **Level 2 (Active / Dragging / Floating Sheet):** Used for lifted tasks and action menus:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.03), 0 16px 36px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08);`

### Glassmorphism & Frosted Materials
- **Floating Island Navigation:** The bottom navigation bar floats 16px above the home indicator, styled with:
  `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);`
  `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%);`
  `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5);`
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.06);`
- **Contextual Overlays:** Modal backgrounds feature a low-density neutral veil (`rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.25)`) accompanied by a gentle `blur(8px)` backdrop blur to maintain contextual awareness without sensory shock.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, rounded geometry inspired by smooth beach pebbles and industrial Scandinavian furniture. 

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Modular Cards & Panels:** Configured with `rounded-2xl` (16px to 20px) to form organic, welcoming modules.
- **Sheets & Modal Containers:** Formed with `rounded-3xl` (24px to 28px) at top junctions to communicate friendly ergonomics when pulled upwards.
- **Micro Elements (Pills, Chips, Avatars, Checkmarks):** Pure pill shape (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for status indicators, counter badges, tags, and action pills.
- **Input Fields & Segmented Controls:** Balanced at `rounded-xl` (12px) to convey stability without appearing boxy or rigid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1E293B` background, pure `#FFFFFF` label, `rounded-xl` or full pill, zero border, active scale state of `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.1)` border, `#121316` label, subtly filling with `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.03)` on press.
- **Floating New Item CTA:** 56px circular or pill button, subtle `#1E293B` or Sage tint, elevated with Level 2 diffusion.

### Checkmarks (Micro-interactions)
- Circular 22px interactive rings.
- **Unchecked:** `1.5px solid #D1D5DB` with pure white center.
- **Hover/Touch Press:** Ring contracts slightly, transitioning border color to Botanical Sage (`#2A7E5D`).
- **Completed:** Instantly fills with `#2A7E5D`, animating a crisp white check vector while striking through adjacent typography with `#9499A5`.

### Segmented Controls
- Continuous track filled with `#F1F2F6`, inset padding of 3px, `rounded-xl`.
- **Active Segment:** Pure white card sliding underneath the text, elevated with `0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`, label weighted in Medium/Semibold `#121316`.

### Cards & Task Rows
- **List Task Item:** Flat or minimal surface, 52px minimum height, structured with checkmark on the left, primary task text, optional Terracotta priority dot (6px) or Indigo category pill, and muted time label on the right.
- **Modular Summary Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl`, Level 1 ambient elevation, 16px internal padding, containing progress ring bars or quick-entry widgets.

### Input Fields
- Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F4F7` background, `rounded-xl`, hairline border (`1px solid #E8E8ED`).
- Focus state activates a subtle Slate stroke (`#1E293B`) without aggressive outer glow rings.

### Pills & Badges
- Capsule radius (`rounded-full`), height 24px, horizontal padding of 8px.
- Subtle tinted fill at 10% opacity matching the accent hue (e.g., Indigo `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.10)`), typography set in `label-sm` with full accent intensity (`#6366F1`).
